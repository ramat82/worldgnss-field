P500/P800 NTRIP Tools

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Application: P500/P800 NTRIP Tools
Android package: dz.ntripcaster
Developer: Tabbi Amar — OGEF n°322

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application P500/P800 NTRIP Tools processes information when you use its ProMark 500, ProMark 800 and P500/P800 Caster Bridge functions.

1. No user account

The application does not require you to create an account with the developer.

2. Bluetooth and connected-device information

The application may request Android Bluetooth / nearby-device permissions in order to discover, select and communicate with compatible GNSS receivers. Device names, Bluetooth addresses and connection state are used for the requested connection. The developer does not operate a server that receives this information.

3. GNSS, NMEA and location data

The application processes GNSS/NMEA data received from the connected receiver. This may include coordinates, altitude, quality indicators and NMEA messages such as GGA.

When an NTRIP workflow requires a GGA message, the application may send the GNSS position to the NTRIP caster selected and configured by the user. When TCP/NMEA forwarding is enabled, NMEA data may also be sent to the network endpoint selected by the user. These transfers are necessary to perform the functions explicitly requested by the user and are not sent to a developer-owned tracking server.

4. NTRIP, network and receiver configuration

To provide NTRIP and receiver-management functions, the application may process configuration entered by the user, such as caster host, port, mountpoint, username, password, APN or receiver settings. Saved profiles are stored locally on the device so that the user can reuse them. The application sends connection information only to the external service or receiver selected by the user.

5. Google Play purchases

The Google Play edition offers separate one-time purchases for the P500 Base/Rover module, the P800 Base/Rover module and the P500/P800 Caster Bridge module. Payments are processed by Google Play. The application can receive purchase status, product identifiers and entitlement information required to activate or restore purchased modules. The developer does not receive or store your bank-card details.

6. Local storage and retention

Application settings, trial state, saved connection profiles and other configuration needed for operation may be stored locally on the Android device. Users can remove locally stored application data through Android settings or by uninstalling the application. Information transmitted to a third-party NTRIP caster or other user-selected service is subject to that service's own retention and privacy practices.

7. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal data and does not share app data with advertising networks. Data is transmitted to third parties only when required for a function selected by the user, for example Google Play for purchase processing or an NTRIP caster / network service chosen by the user.

8. Security

The application is designed to keep its working configuration on the user's device and to communicate only with receivers and services selected by the user. Users are responsible for choosing trusted NTRIP casters, network endpoints and credentials.

9. Children

P500/P800 NTRIP Tools is a professional GNSS/surveying utility and is not directed to children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if application features or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this public URL.

11. Contact

For privacy questions concerning P500/P800 NTRIP Tools, use the developer contact information shown on the application's Google Play listing or the official WorldGNSS Field website.
