Worldwide coordinate systems

CRS selection based on the survey location

WorldGNSS Field can detect the country and UTM zone from a WGS84 position and propose coordinate systems relevant to that geographic context.

  • WGS84 is kept as the geographic pivot.
  • National and projected CRS are filtered by survey location.
  • Regional CRS clutter is kept out of the normal field selection.
  • PROJ-backed transformations are used for supported generic EPSG workflows.
  • Project CRS and survey geography are checked for consistency.
